# Dashboard

The first screen when you open the panel. Read-only — no actions, just numbers.

## What you see

- **Counters** — total jobs, gangs, public interactions, items, custom blips / peds, players online.
- **Online by job** — list of currently connected characters grouped by their job. Click a row to see grades and duty state.
- **Recent activity** — the latest entries from the audit log.

## Online players panel

Each row shows:

- Server id.
- Character name.
- Job + grade.
- Gang + grade (if any).
- Duty: on / off.

The list updates live as players connect, disconnect, change job, or toggle duty.

## When you would use it

- A quick "are my cops online?" check.
- Verifying a player got the job after you assigned them one from your admin tools.
- Spotting that paychecks didn't drop (no one is on duty).
